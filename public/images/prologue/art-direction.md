## Prompt final

Create a photorealistic editorial artwork for a Tamil remembrance history website. Portrait 4:5 composition. This is a fictional symbolic evocation, not an archival photo of an actual event. Interior of an ordinary modest northern Sri Lankan home, looking through an open weathered timber doorway into a quiet overgrown tropical courtyard with a distant palmyra palm. Intimate eye-level photography, natural imperfect architecture, aged pale lime plaster with subtle cracks, worn dark wood, dusty reddish cement floor, soft overcast daylight entering the doorway. A simple faded deep crimson cotton cloth hangs loosely from a wooden chair at the right edge, an old closed school exercise book sits on its seat. Empty, inhabited-in-memory, poignant and dignified. Dark charcoal shadows along the left edge allow seamless blending into a black website background. Restrained deep oxblood red, earthy stone, muted olive, warm ivory light. Real camera optics, tactile material detail, nuanced film grain, natural exposure, depth and quiet human absence. Full-frame environmental photograph, not a drawing, not 3D, not a floating illustration or icon. No people, no bodies, no weapons, no blood, no lamp, no candles, no text, no logos, no dramatic fire or destruction. Save the image to a local file and provide its path.
